# Topic 34 — Forensic Readiness and Standardisation in Organisations
*(Course catalogue topic 34. Handbook title: "Validated Digital-Forensic Readiness Maturity Model for Organizations".)*
Source: CS616 Two-Track Handbook, pp. 29–30 · Track A (Review) only.

**Research problem.** Applying a checklist to one scenario is an assessment exercise. A research contribution requires transparent model construction, independent scoring, scenario validation, and evidence that **maturity levels predict investigative capability**.

Catalogue framing: whether an organisation can later produce admissible evidence is decided **before** an incident; the topic examines forensic-readiness frameworks — logging, retention, time synchronisation, relevant ISO standards — and how an organisation's maturity can be assessed.

## Track A — Systematic Review: Digital-Forensic Readiness Frameworks and Maturity Models
**Review questions**
- **RQ1.** Which forensic-readiness frameworks and maturity models exist (Rowlingson; ISO/IEC 27043/27035; NIST/NCA guidance)?
- **RQ2.** Which readiness capabilities are supported by standards and practice, and how is readiness measured?
- **RQ3.** Is there evidence that maturity predicts investigative capability, and where are the gaps?

**Scope & search protocol.** Databases **and standards bodies**. String:
`("forensic readiness" OR "digital forensic readiness") AND (maturity OR framework OR standard OR ISO)`. Window **2001–2026**. **PRISMA-style flow; standards reviewed directly.**

**Synthesis & appraisal.** Taxonomy of capabilities; matrix (**framework, standard basis, measurement method, validation**); appraise reliability/validation evidence; map gaps.

**Expected contribution.** A synthesis of readiness capabilities grounded in standards and of how (rarely) maturity instruments are validated, with a **measurement agenda**.

**Handbook references (verified):**
1. R. Rowlingson, "A ten step process for forensic readiness," *Int. J. Digital Evidence*, vol. 2, no. 3, 2004 (foundational).
2. ISO/IEC 27043:2015 *Incident investigation principles and processes*; ISO/IEC 27037:2012 *Digital evidence handling*; ISO/IEC 27035 series *Information security incident management*.
3. ISO/IEC 30121:2015 *Governance of digital forensic risk framework*.
4. NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3, *Incident Response Recommendations and Considerations for Cybersecurity Risk Management*, 2025.
5. G. Horsman, "The importance of digital evidence strategies," *WIREs Forensic Science*, vol. 6, no. 1, e1507, 2024.
